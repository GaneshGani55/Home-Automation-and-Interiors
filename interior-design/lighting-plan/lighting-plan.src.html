Verdict (accepted design, 2026-10-02): the layered scheme works without any downlight in the 21 ft void. Two changes from the check were accepted and are built in: the half-landing fixture is now an Avia 7 W aimed at the stair wall (the Bolt 12 W would have made a ≈ 1,170 lx pool in climbers' eyes), and the pendant takes 806 lm smart bulbs. Order the Axios heads as 38° (stair), 24° (TV wall), 60° (curtain).
